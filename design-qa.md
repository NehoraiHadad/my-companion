## Full-view comparison evidence

The implementation preserves the selected art direction: deep midnight palette, warm room light, a high-quality 3D companion as the primary focus, four visible care meters, large rounded care controls, and a persistent bottom navigation surface. The implementation intentionally reallocates some of the source mock's oversized hero/action area to game state, speech, camera access, four direct care actions, and navigation required by the working game.

## Focused-region evidence

`design-qa-focus.png` enlarges the needs/character/care region. It confirms that icon weight is consistent, the character remains legible against the room, the status hierarchy reads in Hebrew RTL, and the four care actions stay distinct without overlap. A focused crop was required because the full-view comparison makes the small meter labels difficult to judge.

## Required fidelity surfaces

- Fonts and typography: Roboto/Segoe UI fallback renders Hebrew cleanly. Name, stage, metric values, labels and action labels have distinct optical weights. No broken wrapping or truncation was observed in the tested default state.
- Spacing and layout rhythm: 16px page margins, compact 7–14px internal gaps, 20–23px radii and consistent card elevation form a stable rhythm. Persistent controls remain separated from the character and status strip.
- Colors and tokens: midnight navy, coral/amber accent, muted slate copy and translucent panels closely preserve the selected source palette. Active, urgent, disabled, connected and reward states are semantically distinct.
- Image quality and asset fidelity: room and companion are real generated raster assets, not CSS or placeholder art. Character edges, crop and shadow are clean at the mobile size; Radix icons are used consistently.
- Copy and content: Hebrew copy is coherent and standalone. Humor is short and contextual. OpenRouter consent and the ChatGPT-subscription limitation are stated plainly.
- Accessibility and resilience: semantic buttons/labels, practical tap targets, reduced-motion support, contrast and explicit disabled states are present. Settings and AI are full-height scrollable pages, avoiding the earlier bottom-sheet clipping.
